## Hi, I'm Ranveer

Final year Computer Science student at City St George's in London.

I like building things and understanding how they work underneath, especially the performance side: memory, efficiency, and working out why something is slower than it should be. I got plenty of that over the past year at Feral Interactive, working in a large C++ codebase on the iOS and Android port of Tomb Raider. Outside of that I mostly write Python and Java.

Right now I'm digging into SAT solvers for my final year project, trying to figure out why the VSIDS heuristic works as well as it does. I'm also learning Rust on the side, mostly out of curiosity about how its ownership model compares to managing memory by hand.
